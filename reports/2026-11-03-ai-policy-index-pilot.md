## Result

- **Newly observed adopted-rule omissions:** Norwegian Bokmål Wikipedia’s AI guideline and Japanese Wikipedia’s G11 speedy-deletion criterion.
- **Other newly observed omissions:** Arabic and Hungarian proposals, and a Spanish discussion convention.
- **Six continuing omissions** from the first pilot: two guidelines, two proposals, one essay, and the English Wiktionary status question. Five source revisions were unchanged. Commons had a newer RfC revision but its consolidated proposal text and pending confirmation status were unchanged.
- **No earlier omission confirmed covered** in this recheck. The prior Wikifunctions draft is outside the current scope and was not rechecked.

The [Meta index](https://meta.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?oldid=31156026) was still at revision 31156026, dated 12 October, on retrieval and recheck. Comparisons used the full index wikitext and interwiki links, including its discussion and media sections. The entries below are catalogue gaps; first observation does not date adoption. A complete inventory comparison was not possible.
